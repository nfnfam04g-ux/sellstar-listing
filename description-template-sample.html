This is an unopened, brand new Leica M 70th Anniversary Book.

Condition: New, unused. It was purchased as new and has never been used.

Published to commemorate the 70th anniversary of the Leica M system, this is a valuable photo book covering its entire history. It was reportedly very difficult to even reserve in Japan, and physical store stock was extremely limited.

Spanning over 250 pages, this book delves deeply into the history of the M-system Leica.

It features:

  • Insightful essays
  • Historic masterpiece photographs
  • Valuable archival materials
  • Detailed commentary on successive Leica M models
  • The evolution of the Leica M system over 70 years written from multiple perspectives

Dr. Andreas Kaufmann, owner and Chairman of the Supervisory Board of Leica Camera AG, stated about this book: "This is a book of great gratitude to everyone who loves the Leica M system—the 'Leica Family,' including photographers, employees, and users."
